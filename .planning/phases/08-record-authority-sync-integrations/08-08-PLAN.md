Output: `OperatingPicture.tsx`'s Tasks Card renders a real, working `<TaskList incidentId={selectedId} />` when (and only when) an incident marker is selected; a new Playwright assertion proves the fix end-to-end and proves the card is absent for resource/facility selections.
</objective>
